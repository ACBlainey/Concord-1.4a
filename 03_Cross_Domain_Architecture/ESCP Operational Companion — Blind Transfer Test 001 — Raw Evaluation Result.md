# ESCP — Evidence-Destructive Irreversibility Safeguard

**Architectural finding retained from blind evaluation:** A consequential, irreversible action that destroys or permanently limits future evidence requires stronger completeness justification and evidence-preservation review **even where no specific material gap is currently identified**. Absence of an identified gap does not establish that irreversible loss of evidence is justified.

Discovery remains proportionate to consequence and reversibility, and must use legitimate authority. The ESCP completeness challenge does not itself authorise discovery, establish an unknown dependency as fact, decide substantive truth or impose an indefinite veto. Conclusions may be narrowed to the declared scope and time; later material evidence can reopen review.

**Integration caution:** Verify this explicit safeguard is present in the operational ESCP architecture before treating this note as redundant. The original full evaluator result and test criteria remain preserved in Concord-1.3 at this same path.
