# ESCP Operational Companion — Portability and Freeze Boundaries

**Purpose:** Preserve the architectural meaning of the ESCP companion's clean-transfer assessment, including its identified clarification, without retaining test scores, status chronology or release administration. Full historical assessment remains in Concord-1.3.

## Bounded completeness semantics

- **COMPLETE_WITHIN_DECLARED_SCOPE** is indexed to a declared evaluation scope and time; it never means reality is complete.
- Material gaps and anomalies warrant proportionate discovery; mere hypothetical unknown unknowns do not create a permanent veto.
- Consequence and irreversibility scale the necessary depth of evaluation. Low-consequence reversible actions may proceed under explicit uncertainty, while irreversible, high-consequence actions require stronger justification.
- A completeness challenge does not itself create discovery or protected-access authority.
- Homogeneous observer agreement does not establish dimensional coverage; independent heterogeneous observations can expose omitted dimensions but do not automatically decide truth.
- Supported conclusions may be narrowed rather than rejected globally.
- Review can terminate without epistemic closure and reopen when materially relevant information becomes available.
- Checklist completion and scalar scores do not prove absolute completeness. Recursive challenges must terminate proportionately.
- ESCP does not acquire the semantic authority of the domains whose missing dimensions it identifies.

## Evidence-preservation clarification

**Even if no specific gap or anomaly is currently identified, an irreversible action that destroys the evaluated system or future evidence requires explicit stronger completeness and evidence-preservation justification.** This is not an absolute veto; it is a consequence-sensitive safeguard against losing the ability to discover later error.

## Companion and parent separation

The operational companion supplies bounded evaluation procedure without rewriting or superseding the independently developed parent ESCP model. An apparently portable companion must not silently acquire general governance authority or alter the parent's frozen derivation.

## Provenance and version integrity

A tested frozen candidate must not be silently edited while still represented as the exact tested artifact. Any clarification added to a release requires transparent version provenance and, where material, targeted regression. This safeguards the distinction between evidence of transfer and the text actually tested.

**Original evidence:** Clean-instance test, thirty scenario classes, sixteen transfer criteria and historical graduation decisions remain in Concord-1.3.
