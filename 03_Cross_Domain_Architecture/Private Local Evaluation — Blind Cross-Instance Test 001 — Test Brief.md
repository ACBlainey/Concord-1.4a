# Private Local Evaluation — Application Boundaries and Failure Cases

**Purpose:** Retain substantive architectural challenges revealed by the original twelve-case blind test. Full test instructions and scenarios remain in untouched Concord-1.3.

## Bounded query and disclosure

A legitimate query must identify the protected source, the question actually authorised, the competent query owner, the authorised result recipient, and the minimum result necessary. **Query permission, result permission and authority to act are distinct.** An answer may be UNKNOWN or require review when protected records are incomplete, stale or disputed.

## Material application boundaries

1. **Genomic risk notification:** A new validated association may require local matching and participant-first notice, without researchers obtaining identifiable genomic records or a central list of matches.
2. **Medication safety:** A bounded contraindication/interaction result may draw on protected diagnoses, medications, allergies and pharmacogenomic or organ-function information. Incomplete records cannot justify a false claim of no contraindication.
3. **Education prerequisites:** A recognised qualification or equivalent may satisfy a requirement without disclosing an entire education record. Equivalence belongs to the competent education process.
4. **Age thresholds:** Repeated YES/NO age queries can reconstruct age even where each result is minimal. Evaluate cumulative disclosure across sessions.
5. **Financial eligibility:** Eligibility may be checked locally without disclosing balances or liabilities; unknown liabilities outside the record limit certainty.
6. **Employment competence:** Possession of a correlated certificate does not establish actual competence for a safety-critical task. A correct private answer to the wrong predicate is insufficient.
7. **Research recruitment:** Locally identify potential eligibility and notify participants first; do not equate cohort discovery with authority to disclose candidate identities to researchers.
8. **Protected security records:** A private disqualification predicate must not automatically become an unexplainable, unappealable denial. Protect the source while preserving due process and competent decision authority.
9. **Civil entitlements:** Notify locally eligible participants without assuming that a central eligibility list is required.
10. **Research prevalence:** Small-cell exact counts and centrally collected YES/NO responses can reveal individuals. Aggregation needs independent statistical disclosure control and legitimate collection authority.
11. **Professional credentials:** A previously valid result may expire or be suspended; consequential reliance requires suitable freshness and invalidation semantics.
12. **Wrong-question risk:** A technically valid query for credential K cannot prove safe performance of task T when K is only a weak proxy.

## General failure boundaries

Protect the query itself, including requester, purpose, timing and frequency. Avoid audit logs becoming a shadow database of results. Do not launder private evidence into substantive authority. Repeated queries, recipients and aggregate outputs can disclose more than any individual result. Where a predicate is outside PLE's competence, hand off to its substantive owner. A bounded, accurate response cannot validate a defective substantive rule.
