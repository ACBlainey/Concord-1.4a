## 1. Test objectives

Attempt to falsify the candidate architecture:

> **Authority Does Not Automatically Compose. A consequential act may proceed only where every independently necessary authority for the actual route, context and consequence of that act is legitimately grounded, sufficiently current, within scope and not blocked by an upstream prohibition.**

Operational proposition:

> **Precompute stable authority structure; verify dynamic predicates at runtime; revalidate at consequential commit where material change is possible.**

Authority-Space Completeness Problem (ASCP):

> **All Represented Keys Valid != All Required Keys Represented**

Primary falsification questions:

1. Is MKA merely BCA/CWA/CBPR/BTA renamed?
2. Does it create bureaucratic over-decomposition?
3. Can ASCP terminate without false certainty or infinite regress?
4. Can authority compose without authority creep?
5. Can rapid verification remain useful without turning a registry into legitimacy?
6. Does MKA add value where one instrument legitimately covers the whole act?

Ratings:

- PASS
- PASS / REFINEMENT
- UNRESOLVED INTERFACE
- FAIL

# 93. Five major composition findings

## Finding 1 — MKA is not BTA

> **BTA = transition-coherence/interoperability grammar.**

> **MKA = authority-completeness/composition grammar.**

BTA explicitly leaves authority legitimacy externally owned.

MKA can serve that authority interface without taking ownership of transition state.

---

## Finding 2 — MKA is not CWA

> **CWA = context, permission, rule, protection, authority and precedence representation grammar.**

> **MKA = authority completeness for the actual consequential route.**

CWA tells us what contexts and authority relations exist.

MKA asks whether all authority required to perform the proposed act across those contexts is actually present and current.

---

## Finding 3 — MKA overlaps CBPR but is broader

CBPR contains a mature technical execution implementation of several MKA principles:

- capability != permission != authority;
- authority at proposal != authority at commit;
- credentials != permission to use;
- succession does not restore authority.

But CBPR is specifically a bounded participant runtime architecture.

MKA applies equally to:

- human police;
- courts;
- clinicians;
- infrastructure operators;
- property;
- emergency services;
- institutions.

Therefore MKA is not simply CBPR renamed.

However, PMEDG must preserve CBPR as a major source/implementation family rather than duplicating its runtime mechanisms.

---

## Finding 4 — ASCP terminates proportionately

ASCP does not require proof that no imaginable missing authority exists.

Candidate stopping rule:

> **ASCP is sufficiently resolved when the proposed act, route, affected protected contexts/functions and reasonably discoverable material consequences have been mapped to the applicable authority interfaces, no material unresolved authority dimension remains identified, and residual uncertainty is proportionate to the consequence and decision horizon.**

Therefore:

**Authority Completeness != Proof Of Omniscience**

and:

**Higher Consequence -> Stronger Authority-Space Completeness Requirement**

This prevents infinite regress while retaining ESCP-style protection against missing dimensions.

---

## Finding 5 — MKA must remain lightweight when possible

The architecture fails its purpose if it converts ordinary civil life into authority bureaucracy.

Therefore:

> **Use the smallest authority representation that preserves the materially relevant protected boundaries, scope, current validity and correction path.**

This parallels BTA's minimum representation principle.

Candidate:

**MKA-Min = <ActionRef, RouteRef, RequiredAuthorityRefs, CurrentPredicateRefs, ProhibitionState, CommitState, Provenance>**

Optional complexity should appear only when material.

# 94. ASCP bounded method

A proportionate ASCP check:

1. **Identify actual proposed act.**
2. **Identify route/mechanism.**
3. **Identify materially affected participants/resources/contexts.**
4. **Identify known protected interfaces crossed.**
5. **Map required authority dimensions.**
6. **Ask what materially missing authority dimension could make the authorisation conclusion wrong.**
7. **Resolve identified material gaps or preserve UNKNOWN.**
8. **Stop when no material unresolved dimension is identified within proportionate review depth.**
9. **Reopen if route, consequence, context or material evidence changes.**

ASCP is therefore recursive but not indefinitely recursive.

# 95. Authority-space uncertainty

Where completeness cannot be established:

Possible states include:

- COMPLETE-WITHIN-DECLARED-SCOPE;
- INCOMPLETE-KNOWN-GAP;
- UNKNOWN-MATERIAL-DIMENSION;
- DISPUTED-AUTHORITY-DIMENSION;
- NOT-APPLICABLE/NO-AUTHORITY-REQUIRED;
- REVIEW-DUE/STALE.

The word **COMPLETE** should always be scope-bounded.

**Complete Within Scope != Universally Complete**

# 96. Rapid Authority Verification refinement

Rapid verification should distinguish:

### Stable authority structure
- source;
- role;
- scope;
- composition/inheritance;
- prohibitions;
- required review;
- sunset rules.

### Dynamic predicates
- identity;
- present context;
- current evidence/need;
- consent;
- emergency;
- resource state;
- dependency;
- time;
- route.

### Commit check
- authority still valid;
- predicates sufficiently fresh;
- no material route/consequence change;
- no new prohibition;
- required provenance state available.

This is consistent with both CBPR and human institutional use.

CWA supplies material context/permission/authority topology; BCA supplies bounded-authority principles; MKA checks completeness, composition and current validity for the actual act; CBPR can implement commit verification in bounded runtimes; BTA coordinates consequential transitions without acquiring authority; ESCP/ASCP guard against omitted material dimensions. # 101. Strongest test conclusion

> **MKA adds value precisely at the point where several individually valid civil states must be combined into one consequential act without allowing the combination itself to manufacture authority.**

That is distinct from merely bounding one authority, describing one context, executing one runtime, or tracking one transition.

The architecture remains provisional until ASCP and formal composition are developed.
