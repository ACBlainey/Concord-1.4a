# GTP irregular-node topology — outstanding implementation requirements (042)

For integration into Governance V2.2 §§132.0c–d, outer-ring topology validation must explicitly address disconnected incident fans, holes and nonmanifold topology, spherical tessellation defects and edge consistency. Distinguish combinatorial mesh fixtures from admissible physical spherical GTP tessellations; an altered fixture is not evidence of a realistic spherical defect or valid physical mesh. No authorial decision is inferred that all possible irregular node topologies must be accepted as valid governance geometry.

Other candidate-ring, selection, identity, seam, quorum and authority requirements remain in V2.2 Chapter 132 and the applicable voting rules. Full historical synthetic test remains in Concord-1.3 at this same path.
