# Governance V2.1 — Unresolved Cross-Chapter Interfaces (from Test 006)

The complete independent arithmetic validation, 19,683-case result matrix, scenario crosswalk and development/graduation history remain unchanged at this exact path in Concord-1.3 (original blob `8380b562753675b143768e34f8a353ccd53e8ceb`). The arithmetic and already-settled governance rules are not repeated here. This document retains only interface questions requiring explicit owner resolution; it grants no authority.

1. **Institutional versus geometric escalation (C-006-01).** Original Chapter 12 §§12.4/12.11 use “expand participation” and “vertical escalation” terminology. Distinguish legitimate constitutional institutional escalation from GTP geometry, which grants no hierarchical authority. Chapter 151's constitutional federal hierarchy must not be silently erased.
2. **Quorum and unknown result (C-006-02).** A two-matching-position arithmetic resultant does not independently establish legal quorum, eligibility or jurisdiction. An unknown/undetermined outcome must not be conflated with vacancy, abstention or procedural invalidity. Resolve the precise quorum and eligibility interface with the voting state machine.
3. **Origin-included independence (C-006-03).** Specify whether one origin Steward may participate in more than one constituent triad in the origin-included procedure, and enforce participant independence and one-position-per-decision-event rules without inventing authority. Repeated influence can alter the virtual result.
4. **Geometry-to-office mapping (C-006-04).** Specify how the six-Steward node office maps to triangle-owned positions, including topology and eligibility, rather than inferring seats from geometry.
5. **Binding common competence (C-006-05).** Coordination across GTP cells is not binding jurisdiction. Define the independently authorised common-competence instrument and applicable precedence rules; a larger cell or neighbouring majority does not inherit command.
6. **Emergency triggers (C-006-06).** An unresolved vote does not itself create emergency power. Specify independently lawful threshold, authorisation, expiry and review for preservation, separate from decision arithmetic.

These are outstanding interface-design questions, not evidence that the V2.2 operational architecture is absent in its entirety. Resolve against the current V2.2 and competent cross-domain owners before integration.
