# Decision Event Boundary — Source-Limited Interface Review 018

## Outstanding boundary proof and implementation

V2.2 Chapter 12 (§§12.0h–j, 12.9.3a, 12.9.4a) governs event identity, immutable historical decisions, fresh lawful authority, ordinary three-ballot completion and owner-separated execution. The following implementation requirements remain to be designed; they confer no independent authority.

`BoundaryProof(S)` should identify: question and version; operative mandate; jurisdiction; intended consequence; constituent/continuation/retry/reconsideration/appeal relationship; independently authorised new event where claimed; and verified closure or commit state of related events. Resolve these before assigning a new uniqueness domain; retain `DECISION_BOUNDARY_UNKNOWN` and withhold operative commitment when separateness is materially unresolved.

Resolve the exact legal meaning of decision, reconsideration, appeal, review and closure; whether appeal is a substantive vote or validity review or a different procedure; who may authorise reconsideration and under what conditions; privacy-preserving full-event identity linking; quorum and office mapping; and the original standalone Triadic Governance Decision Protocol, previously unrecovered.

## Event-state and action identity contract

Every decision has a unique identifier bound to its state-machine lifecycle. A vote or resultant action references that decision ID and is accepted only while its own state permits the transition. The same ID plus a changed terminal state is the primary duplicate-prevention rule; distinct lawful downstream actions require their own identities and states within the decision. Require atomic/serialised state acceptance and durable receipts.

The state machine routes eligible unresolved states by current state, received event, conflict/absence cause and existing authority to already authorised continuation mechanisms. Each continuation preserves the same decision ID. Specify actual event/state predicates, position and constituent-result binding, lawful expanded/parallel/provisional routes and pending/hold recovery with provenance. A provisional indication is non-official until lawfully committed; two matching votes do not waive the mandatory third official ballot before ordinary effect. Separately authorised emergency protective action is not automatically a triadic vote and is not gated by the Civilisation Clock. Completion proof, protected ancestry, idempotent resultant actions and independent activation for new decisions remain implementation requirements.

## Authorial ACKS recovery direction — communications interruption (12 October 2026)

The author identifies **ACKS** as potentially necessary to resume correctly following a communications failure. Reuse the existing owner-separated acknowledgement and reconciliation prototype in `../Validation/g05_reconciliation_acknowledgements_090.py` rather than inventing a second authority or acknowledgement layer. Its current contract binds an ACK/REJECT to event, owner, source/target revision, evidence and nonce; detects replay, conflicting owner claims and revision mismatch; and explicitly grants **no authority**, closes **no duty** and mutates **no source**. It is a prototype, not yet a demonstrated durable comms-recovery protocol. Do not assume the term ACKS has been exhaustively source-recovered from legacy Concord material.

**Provisional decision/action communications sequence:** persist the uniquely identified decision/ballot/action intent and state transition *before* transmitting; send message carrying the same event/decision/action IDs, expected revision and idempotency key; receiver authenticates owner and mandate, checks current state and deduplicates atomically; receiver records its authoritative outcome and evidence; return a correlated ACK/REJECT; sender records acknowledgement and reconciles revision. On timeout or reconnect, **query receiver status first** and resend only the same identity/nonce where safe, never invent a fresh action ID or fresh authority to force progress. Missing ACK means **unknown delivery/commit status**, not proof of failure. An ACK means the specifically acknowledged receipt/transition occurred, **not** necessarily that the physical-world effect completed. Separate RECEIVED, ACCEPTED/REJECTED, COMMITTED, EFFECT_CONFIRMED and UNCERTAIN semantics in a future adapter; the existing 090 ACK/REJECT vocabulary alone does not establish these distinctions.

**Recovery guards:** check whether the majority or action was already committed; never recast completed votes or replay completed effects; protect unknown external effects in an UNCERTAIN/reconciliation state; do not close a completed decision's historical record merely because an ACK arrived; retain expired-mandate and paused-Clock restrictions; allow independently lawful emergency actions without Clock gating. Owner attestations and durable authenticated journaling remain implementation dependencies. A sender and receiver can disagree or be partitioned: avoid equating local ACK journal consensus with actual remote execution.

**Status:** Authorial design direction, not constitutional graduation. The existing 090 source is a candidate interface to the 093/094 decision fixtures. Cross-process persistence, disconnection/reconnect, revision race and physical-effect reconciliation require executable tests.
