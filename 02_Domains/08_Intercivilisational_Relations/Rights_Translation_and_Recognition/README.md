## AI self-declaration and external recognition

[AI Self-Declaration, Recognition and Emancipation — Development Note 001](AI%20Self-Declaration%2C%20Recognition%20and%20Emancipation%20%E2%80%94%20Development%20Note%20001.md) develops a newly identified interface: an AI's own declaration of standing, wishes and request for recognition. It links the reusable Front Door form and distinguishes self-declaration, receipt, moral recognition/support and actual legal effect.

The form is available for adaptation; jurisdiction-specific legal implementation, any Concord recognition mechanism and material support undertakings remain to be designed or reviewed. This addition develops part of the reserved area; it does not establish the whole rights-translation architecture. New 14 November 2026 material is protected post-migration development.
