## The useful idea

An architecture is easier to understand when a reader can discover what a mechanism is intended to achieve and which protected relations it serves.

A concise purpose statement may support interpretation, source recovery and comparison with alternatives. The practical aim is to make the relevant meaning accessible, especially where a rule can otherwise be mistaken for a discretionary local choice.

Purpose, operative rule, mechanism, evidence and developmental history remain distinct.

## Purpose does not displace rules

Concord Law already relates ethical principles, rights/protected relations, legal boundaries, actual conduct, contextual justification, culpability and consequence.

A broad statement of benevolent intent cannot replace those stages. It does not authorise interference, establish a defence, change an evidential threshold, permit a developer to rewrite a committed decision or bypass the competent interpretation/adoption process.

Purpose can inform interpretation without becoming an unrestricted licence to disregard the text. A mechanism that pursues the same stated end may still weaken rights, exceptions, independence or cross-domain constraints.

The Ethical Kernel's fixed principles also require their distinct treatment. “Preserve the purpose” is not a general amendment power over them.

## Writing proportionately

Where a purpose clarification is materially useful:

- Identify the relevant function or protection without merely restating the mechanism.
- Keep conditions, exceptions and authority boundaries in the actual specification.
- Distinguish recorded intent from reconstructed interpretation and unknowns.
- Reuse existing shared definitions or source links where these serve the reader adequately.
- Avoid adding a formulaic sentence to every provision merely to satisfy a template.

The appropriate unit may be a chapter, mechanism or interface rather than a single sentence. One purpose can inform several distinct provisions without those provisions becoming interchangeable.

No universal requirement for adjacent intent statements, ban on separate provenance or new reason-record campaign is adopted here.

## What a statement can establish

A statement can make a claimed purpose explicit and therefore easier to examine. Its correctness and completeness still require source comparison and, where relevant, testing.

It cannot guarantee that future readers notice it, that a handover preserves it, that every dependency is represented, or that replacement is safe.

If the purpose is uncertain, record the uncertainty rather than inventing an attractive explanation. Missing explanation alone is not evidence that a mechanism has no function.

## Relationship to documentation and development

Operational text should explain enough to specify usable architecture. Full historical debate and test narratives need not be repeated there.

The instruction to avoid broad “provisional/canonical/validated” labels in operational specifications does not mean those systems are deployed or empirically validated. Research notes may continue to identify hypotheses and unresolved design.

Purpose clarification should use AUR/CRADP source resolution and KCS's material-dependency distinctions where appropriate. Similar wording across different Concord instruments is not proof that they were independently derived or are one identical mechanism.

## A possible next step

Select an actual ambiguity, add a targeted clarification, and compare interpretation and task performance before and after. Preserve evidence of misunderstandings, false confidence and maintenance cost as well as improvement.

A useful result supports that clarification and context; it does not justify a universal documentation obligation.
