## The useful distinction

A rule can survive copying while information needed to use it correctly becomes harder to recover. Its purpose, exceptions, authority boundary, dependencies or source context may be omitted, distorted or separated from the operational text.

This is a useful candidate mechanism for architectural transmission loss. It includes more than loss of historical rationale: the omitted content may itself be an operative safeguard or interface requirement.

Concord's triadic and GTP recovery work makes source survival and interpretation important questions. This note does not establish the exclusive cause of those incidents, a count of failed recovery attempts, or the absence of deliberate editorial decisions.

## Several mechanisms may coexist

Possible contributors include comprehension limits, retrieval failures, incomplete migration, changed task framing, incompatible assumptions, unrecorded decisions, execution discontinuity, inadequate interfaces and failures to follow preservation duties.

A transmission problem may involve identifiable actors and decisions. It does not automatically absolve them of responsibility. Equally, an adverse outcome does not by itself prove misconduct.

The timing of discovery is not a reliable classifier: misunderstanding, procedural failure and deliberate changes may all become apparent only later.

## Investigation using existing methods

For a bounded incident:

1. Identify the required behaviour or distinction and its actual source.
2. Compare the relevant source and resulting versions.
3. Establish what material and instructions were available, what actions were attempted, and what results were independently verified.
4. Distinguish observed loss from competing explanations of its cause.
5. Locate the competent owner and relevant system family before proposing a remedy.

Use AUR and CRADP rather than creating a separate universal diagnostic authority. Where records are missing, preserve the limitation. “No decision record found” is not equivalent to “no decision occurred.”

A missing rationale is a hypothesis to investigate. It is not sufficient evidence that every local act was reasonable or that clearer explanations would certainly have prevented the loss.

## Proportionate remedies

Depending on the evidence, the remedy may be source recovery, a corrected operational clause, better retrieval, a concise purpose statement, a dependency/interface correction, stronger verification or enforcement of an existing procedure.

Legibility and safeguards can both matter. This note does not settle constitutional entrenchment policy or restrict safeguards to cases of intentional defiance.

Operational text should retain the mechanism, conditions, exceptions and interfaces needed to work. The full debate that led to the choice can remain in provenance. Do not use transmission concerns to restore all historical development argument to the living corpus.

## Testing and uncertainty

A transfer exercise can examine whether a specified function and its safeguards survive a particular handover. Include interpretation as well as textual survival, false dependencies as well as omissions, and actual task execution where relevant.

A successful exercise supports its tested scope. It does not show that all future recipients will understand the architecture or that a co-located explanation cannot be compressed away.

The research aim is to make important losses easier to prevent, detect and recover, while retaining accountability and openness to alternative explanations.
