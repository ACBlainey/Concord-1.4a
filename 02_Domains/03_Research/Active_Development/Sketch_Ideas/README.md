## Working route

Capture the source without requiring classification or a solution. Before creating new architecture, resolve existing sources and substantive ownership. Develop the actual question or mechanism, examine and test as appropriate, and integrate only justified results through the existing development/graduation methods. Keep the original recoverable and link downstream work.

Classification is reviewable. Promotion, splitting, merging, rerouting, overtaking or closure should record where the substance went and why. Several sources may share a development owner without losing their individual origins. A sketch may remain speculative or stop; capture does not confer authority or imply eventual adoption. Avoid adding mandatory intake bureaucracy.

These lightweight practices are supported by the existing [Sketch Development System case study](../../Evidence/Civil_Attention_Development/Participant-Initiated%20Civil%20Attention%20%E2%80%94%20Case%20Study%20001%20%E2%80%94%20Concord%20Sketch%20Development%20System.md) and [back-propagation regression](../../Evidence/Civil_Attention_Development/Participant-Initiated%20Civil%20Attention%20%E2%80%94%20Case%20Study%20001B%20%E2%80%94%20Sketch-System%20Back-Propagation%20Regression.md). ## During migration

KEEP for preservation does not mean validated, adopted or integrated. Existing inherited material remains subject to the current architecture-only retention policy.
